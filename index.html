Problem Statement & Context

The Problem

Digital users are increasingly vulnerable to sophisticated AI-generated deepfakes. This information reliability gap causes poor decision-making and erodes community trust in digital media.

📊

Supporting Data

67% of Filipinos express deep concern regarding fake news, and 65% struggle to identify misinformation accurately.

Source: Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2025

Problem Analysis

👥

Stakeholders

Primarily social media users, students, content creators, and local journalists.

📈

Impact

Risk of financial loss via scams and severe damage to professional reputations.

🌳

Root Causes

Generative AI tools outperforming human detection and high friction in current verification methods.
